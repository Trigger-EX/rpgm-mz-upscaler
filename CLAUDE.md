## Autonomy
Work until the task is fully complete. Don't stop to ask for confirmation, report progress, or offer next steps. Make reasonable decisions and note them in the final summary.

End your final message with exactly one of these lines:
- `STATUS: DONE`
- `STATUS: NEEDS_INPUT - <the specific question>`

Only use NEEDS_INPUT when you truly can't proceed: missing credentials, an irreversible/destructive choice, or a genuinely ambiguous requirement.
